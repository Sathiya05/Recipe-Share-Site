Creamy Alfredo Veggie

Classic Italian pasta with creamy garlic sauce and fresh veggies

Ingredients:

  • 200g fettuccine or spaghetti
  • 1 cup broccoli florets, 1/2 cup bell peppers, 1/2 cup carrots
  • 1 cup heavy cream, 1/2 cup Parmesan
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced, salt & pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. Cook pasta until al dente.
  2. Sauté veggies with garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add cream, Parmesan, and seasonings; thicken sauce.
  4. Toss pasta in sauce and garnish with parsley.
Veg Snack 15 min

Spicy Veg Sandwich

Freshly toasted bread with spicy vegetables and tangy sauces

Ingredients:

  • 4 slices bread, buttered
  • 1 small tomato & cucumber, sliced
  • 1/2 cup shredded lettuce, 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 2 tbsp chutney/mayo, chili flakes, salt & pepper

Steps:

  1. Toast buttered bread until golden.
  2. Mix veggies with spices.
  3. Spread chutney/mayo on bread and layer veggies filling.
  4. Cut diagonally & serve with ketchup.
Veg Asian 35 min

Steamed Momos

Soft steamed dumplings with flavorful vegetable filling

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1/4 cup water, pinch of salt
  • 1 cup chopped cabbage, 1/2 cup carrot, 1/4 cup capsicum
  • 1/4 cup spring onions, 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil

Steps:

  1. Make soft dough and rest for 20 mins.
  2. Mix vegetables with soy sauce and sesame oil for filling.
  3. Shape momos and seal edges.
  4. Steam for 10–12 mins and serve with chili sauce.

Ingredients & Steps

Follow the recipe easily with organized tabs for ingredients and steps

  • 200g fettuccine or spaghetti
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced
  • 1/2 cup carrots, julienned
  • 1/2 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt & black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil over medium heat. Add broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, and mushrooms. Cook 4–5 mins.
  3. Pour in heavy cream, add Parmesan, salt, and pepper. Stir and let sauce thicken slightly.
  4. Toss pasta into sauce until coated evenly.
  5. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.
